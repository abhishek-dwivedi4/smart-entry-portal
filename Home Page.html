Grassroots Innovation in Hostel Administration

The Smart Entry Portal was conceived to replace traditional paper registers with a modern, secure, and digital movement management framework.

By connecting student requests, warden approvals, gate verification, and administrative monitoring in real time, the platform minimizes delays and prevents unauthorized movement.

Aligned with the Vande Bharatam vision of self-reliance and technological empowerment, it sets a benchmark for digital campus governance.
